1. Use src/renderer/luma.ts (already exists, owned by architect-opus, DO NOT EDIT) for all luma math.
2. src/renderer/braille.ts — buildBrailleCells(pixels, decodeWidth, decodeHeight, grid): void
   - Each cell covers a 2x4 pixel block. Braille dot bit order (Unicode): (0,0)=0x01 (0,1)=0x02 (0,2)=0x04 (1,0)=0x08 (1,1)=0x10 (1,2)=0x20
     (0,3)=0x40 (1,3)=0x80, where (x,y) is column 0..1, row 0..3 inside the block.
   - mean = rounded mean luma of the 8 pixels. A dot is ON when its luma > mean (strictly). glyph = BRAILLE_BASE + bits.
   - fg = rounded per-channel mean of ON pixels; bg = rounded per-channel mean of OFF pixels.
   - Uniform block (all lumas equal): bits = 0, glyph = BRAILLE_BASE + 0 would draw nothing; instead use glyph GLYPH_SPACE and
     bg = the block mean color (fg = bg). Document this.
3. src/renderer/ascii.ts — buildAsciiCells(pixels, decodeWidth, decodeHeight, grid): void
   - Ramp " .:-=+*#%@" (10 levels, dark to bright). Cell mean color over the 2x4 block; luma of the mean selects
     ramp[min(9, (Y * 10) >> 8)]. fg = cell mean color, bg = 0x000000. (The ASCII look is colored glyphs on black.)
   - Export the ramp; REQ-CAP-03 needs ASCII to work with no Unicode, so glyphs must all be < 0x80.
